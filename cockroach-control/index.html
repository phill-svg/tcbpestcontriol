[01] Species

The cockroaches showing up around Canberra homes.

German cockroach

The smaller, fast-moving cockroach — clearest sign of an indoor infestation. Pantries, kitchens, behind fridges. Multiple generations per year make early treatment critical.

Australian cockroach

The larger reddish-brown cockroach — usually enters from the subfloor or the roof void, especially in autumn. Common in established older homes.

Full species guide

Oriental cockroach

The dark, slower cockroach — drains and damp areas, particularly laundries, bathrooms and subfloor voids.

American cockroach

Larger again, reddish-brown with a pale edge on the pronotum. Less common in the ACT but seen in commercial kitchens and older homes.

[02] Process

Four steps from call-out to a cockroach-free kitchen.

Inspection

We open the kitchen cupboards, lift the fridge, walk the subfloor, the laundry and the bathroom. We identify the species from sightings, droppings and egg cases — and tell you what we saw.

Gel bait placement

Small dots of professional-grade gel applied only to confirmed harbourage. Cockroaches eat, return to harbourage, and transfer bait to other colony members.

Crack-and-crevice spray

Targeted application behind the fridge, under the sink, inside the cupboard hinges and along known harbourage seams. No whole-room fogging.

Written report

Every visit closes with a typed report — what we found, what we used, what to watch for. Covered re-treatment inside the guarantee window if a treated species returns.

[04] Why DIY often fails

What most home cockroach treatments miss.

Aerosol bombs don't reach harbourage

Cockroaches live in wall cavities and behind appliances. Fog reaches the surface only — they survive and recolonise.

One species looks the same as another

They look similar but behave differently. Identifying the species determines where we treat.

Egg cases reset the cycle

A single ootheca can hold 30–40 eggs. We open up harbourage zones and remove visible cases as part of treatment.

Sanitation is part of the answer

We leave clear written guidance on how to break the cycle — sealed bins, pet food storage, drip-tray hygiene, subfloor airflow.

[05] FAQ

The questions Canberra households and venues ask first.

[FAQ- 01 ]

How quickly will I stop seeing cockroaches?

Most treatments reduce visible activity within 24–72 hours. Full colony take-out via gel bait usually completes within 2–3 weeks depending on species and pressure.

[FAQ- 02 ]

Do you fog the house?

No. Whole-room fogging is rarely effective against cockroaches and exposes the household to unnecessary product. We use targeted gel bait and crack-and-crevice spray, applied only where the species harbours.

[FAQ- 03 ]

Are the products safe around kids and pets?

Yes. We use the lowest-toxicity option that does the job. Gel bait is contained in tamper-resistant placement. Re-entry times are on every quote.

[FAQ- 04 ]

Will they come back?

Inside our guarantee window — no. Long-term prevention is about sealing entry points, managing moisture and removing harbourage.

[FAQ- 05 ]

How much does cockroach control cost in Canberra?

Each job is priced individually — residential and commercial sites are both quoted in writing. We send a written quote before any work starts.
