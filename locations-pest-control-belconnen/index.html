[05] FAQ · Belconnen

Questions Belconnen residents ask first.

[FAQ- 01 ]

Are funnel-webs really a risk near Lake Ginninderra?

Yes — the bushland reserves bordering the lake are known funnel-web and black house spider habitat. We target garden beds, retaining walls and under-floor entry points on properties backing onto reserve land, not just the interior.

[FAQ- 02 ]

I'm in a unit near Westfield Belconnen — is treatment different?

The approach is lighter-touch: perimeter and common-area treatment coordinated with your body corporate, rather than the full exterior barrier we'd run on a stand-alone block near the lake.

[FAQ- 03 ]

How do I know if my block is old enough to need a termite inspection?

Most call-outs come from the original lake-side streets and the older pockets around Aranda and Evatt, where timber-framed construction is more common. If your home was built before the mid-1990s, an annual inspection is worth booking.
