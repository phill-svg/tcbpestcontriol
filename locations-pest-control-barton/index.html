[02] Local pests · Barton

The pests we see most in Barton.

Barton is Canberra's government and diplomatic quarter — office buildings, embassies and hotels grouped tight around Parliament House. Almost all our Barton work is commercial, and four issues account for the bulk of it.

Paper pests in archive rooms

Records rooms, basements and quiet storage across Barton's offices are ideal for silverfish and stored-product pests that chew quietly through documents. We treat the harbourage and advise on how the paper is stored.

Ants & spiders at the perimeter

Landscaped surrounds and paved plazas let ants and spiders build up right against the building line before they get in. A perimeter program keeps that pressure off the entries.

Cockroaches in staff kitchens

Function spaces, tea points and catering areas offer cockroaches food and warmth year-round. Discreet scheduled servicing keeps them out of sight of staff and visitors.

Rodents in docks & car parks

Loading bays and basement car parks are the usual way rats and mice get into Barton's larger buildings. Bait stations and proofing at those entry points shut the route down.
