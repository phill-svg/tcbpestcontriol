[02] Local pests · Casey

The pests we see most in Casey.

Casey is one of Gungahlin's newest suburbs, built out through the 2010s on the northern edge against the Mulligans Flat and Goorooyarroo woodland reserves. New homes mean current termite barriers — but that woodland border brings its own pressure. These four come up most on Casey jobs.

Ants & spiders in new gardens

Fresh landscaping, new lawns and paved courtyards across Casey are the first places local ants and black house spiders move into. We treat them inside and out with pet-safe product.

Woodland edge & rodents

Backing onto the Mulligans Flat woodland, Casey's reserve-side streets see field mice and the odd rat move in from the grassland as it cools, plus more spider activity than the inner estates. Treatment focuses on the exterior and the ways in.

Termites down the track

Casey's homes are recent enough that their barriers are still doing their job — but timber framing ages, so a baseline inspection now and a re-check every few years stops subterranean termites starting unnoticed.

Wasps on new fence lines

European wasps take to the new colorbond fences and eaves across Casey each summer — we identify the nest and remove it safely rather than knocking it about.
